How to draft

  • 3 pts; with Tamayo (+2 PWR) or next to a giant.

Game plan

  • Hide the fighter their big special is aimed at; it moves first, so it always lands in time.

Win with it

  • Concealment Talisman moves first: hide an ally (or himself) so attacks miss them this round. Hide whoever their big special is aimed at.
  • With Tamayo he gets +2 PWR.

How to beat it

  • Area specials still hit hidden fighters' teammates; spread damage on his special rounds.
  • Kill him first and their carry has no hiding place.
