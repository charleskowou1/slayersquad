“Why do you torment the weak?!”

Passive

Upper Moon 4’s strongest emotion. His hatred grows: +2 PWR at the end of every round, up to +4. Knock him down and the real Hantengu comes out and hides behind card 1, 2 or 3: to hit him you pick a card. Right card, it lands; wrong card, it misses. Specials that hit every enemy pass over him, and his neck is hard as steel (DEF 10). If the real one survives the round, Zohakuten gets back up, weaker each time. Kill the real one and Zohakuten crumbles. After sunrise Zohakuten stays down and the real one has nowhere to hide.

How to draft

  • 9 pts; with another Upper Moon (Doma is his best partner).

Game plan

  • Fight as Zohakuten: his PWR grows every round, so long fights favour him. Fire Wood Dragons whenever it's ready.
  • When he goes down, hide the real one behind a card. Don't fall into a pattern: the other player is reading you, and every wrong guess means Zohakuten gets back up.

Win with it

  • The card is Zohakuten: a full fighter whose hatred adds +2 PWR a round (up to +4), and Wood Dragons hit every enemy through Defend.
  • When he goes down, the real Hantengu hides behind one of three cards. You pick it in secret every time: vary it, the other player is trying to read you.
  • If the real one survives the round, Zohakuten gets back up (weaker each time), so every wrong guess buys you another life.
  • Doma is his best partner.

How to beat it

  • Knock Zohakuten down, then commit fighters to guessing: two different cards is two chances in three, three covers them all.
  • A wrong guess only costs that fighter's move. Two fighters on the same card is the gamble for a double hit.
  • His neck is hard as steel (DEF 10): save specials that ignore DEF (Hinokami Kagura, Ninth Form) for the real one. Specials that hit every enemy pass over him.

Synergies and matchups

Rival

Facing Hantengu, Mitsuri gets +2 PWR.

Against: Mitsuri Kanroji, Mitsuri Kanroji (Mark)

Unlock: win the Story battle “Splitting Emotions”.
