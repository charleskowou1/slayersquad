Passive

Cut him and it gets worse. The first hit cuts his head off and Sekido and Karaku burst out; the first of them to fall brings Aizetsu and Urogi. When the last emotion falls they merge into Zohakuten, and the real Hantengu hides. Hunt him (guess where he is; a wrong guess leaves that fighter wide open), or knock Zohakuten down to expose him for a round (if the real one survives it, Zohakuten gets back up, +2 PWR stronger each time). Cut down the real one to win.

How to draft

  • 9 pts; with another Upper Moon (Doma is his best partner).

Game plan

  • The emotions are small: use their specials while they stand (Lightning Staff and Gale Fan hit everyone).
  • As Zohakuten, pound Drums of Hatred on quiet rounds and fire Wood Dragons when ready. Every round the real one stays hidden, Zohakuten can get back up, +2 PWR stronger each time.

Win with it

  • Cut him and it gets worse: the first hit cuts his head off and Sekido and Karaku burst out, the first of them to fall brings Aizetsu and Urogi, and when the last emotion falls they merge into Zohakuten while the real Hantengu hides. Many bodies for 9 pts.
  • Zohakuten has no Defend: Drums of Hatred adds +2 PWR for good, and Wood Dragons hit every enemy for full PWR through Defend.
  • Knocked down, Zohakuten gets back up at the end of the next round unless the real one is cut down first.
  • Doma is his best partner.

How to beat it

  • Area specials (Gyomei, Tengen, Mitsuri, Yoriichi) clear the small emotions fast.
  • The win is the real Hantengu. Knock Zohakuten down and put everyone on the real one the next round; Hunt only when you can afford a fighter left wide open (1 in 3).
  • Damage on the real one stays when he hides again, so even a half-finished round counts.

Unlock: win the Story battle “Splitting Emotions”.
