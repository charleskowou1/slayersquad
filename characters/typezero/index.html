Passive

Six Arms: strikes three times every Attack. Only a puppet: it doesn't count as Yoriichi, so Muzan isn't scared of it. Training partner: Muichiro gets +1 PWR on its team. Poison does nothing to a puppet.

How to draft

  • 4 pts; with Muichiro or Muscle Mice.

Game plan

  • Training Drill on a slow carry (Gyomei, a giant) in round 1, then triple-strike Attacks.

Win with it

  • Strikes three times every Attack (PWR 4 each): great with Muscle Mice (+2 on every strike).
  • Training Drill: an ally gets +2 SPD for the match. With Muichiro: Muichiro +1 PWR.

How to beat it

  • Three small hits are weak against high DEF: armour beats him.
  • Poison does nothing to him: he's a puppet, so don't aim Shinobu here.

Synergies and matchups

Training partner

Yoriichi Type Zero and Muichiro on one team: Muichiro gets +1 PWR.

Same team: Muichiro Tokito, Muichiro Tokito (Mark)

Unlock: win the Story battle “Type Zero”.
