Muichiro Tokito (Mark)

Mark ✦Mist Hashira10 pts

“I’ll forget you soon anyway.”

The Mist Hashira, a prodigy who became a Hashira two months after first holding a sword.

Role: Evasive attacker (Mark)

Play Muichiro Tokito free
34HP
14PWR
13SPD
5DEF

Special: Obscuring Clouds

Moves early. Vanishes into mist (attacks miss him this round) and strikes for PWR+2.

Recharges in 2 rounds · moves early.

“The mist hides everything.”

Passive

Mark awakened: boosted stats and the special recharges 1 round faster.

How to draft

  • 10 pts; a mid pick, weaker than the other 10-pt Marks.

Game plan

  • Mist every other round; Attack in between.

Win with it

  • Mark version: the mist comes back a round sooner.

How to beat it

  • Akaza and Hantengu beat him most often at 20 pts; on his special round, don't waste hits on him.

Synergies and matchups

Training partner

Yoriichi Type Zero and Muichiro on one team: Muichiro gets +1 PWR.

Same team: Yoriichi Type Zero, Muichiro Tokito

Hashira

Two or more Hashira on one team: each Hashira gets +1 PWR.

Corps Master
