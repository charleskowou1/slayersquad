How to draft

  • 4 pts; better at 30 points and inside the Kamaboko trio.

Game plan

  • Hold Thunderclap for a fighter he can finish: he moves first, so it lands before they act.
  • Seventh Form (once unlocked) for the big hit on their tank.

Win with it

  • Always moves first, so Thunderclap and Flash (PWR×2) finishes a hurt enemy before it acts.
  • After story battle Senior Brother his second special, Seventh Form (PWR×3, ignores DEF), alternates with it.
  • Against Kaigaku he gets +2 PWR. Part of Kamaboko Squad with Tanjiro and Inosuke. Much better at 30 pts.

How to beat it

  • 26 HP: one big hit or two small ones drop him. He has no Defend tricks.
  • He strikes first but only once: Defend the target he's likely to finish.
