Kagaya Ubuyashiki

Demon SlayerCorps Master2 pts

“What is eternal is human will.”

Head of the Demon Slayer Corps. He never held a sword, and turned his own death into a trap for Muzan.

Role: Support: heals, Mark awakening

Basic move: Words of Resolve

Instead of attacking: every ally, himself included, heals 2.

Special: Final Resolve

Kagaya gives his life (he's knocked out). Every Hashira on his team who has a Mark awakens it for the rest of the match. If an enemy knocks him out first, nobody awakens.

Recharges in 3 rounds.

“My children, thank you.”

Passive

Master of the Corps: every Hashira on his team gets +1 DEF. He never attacks. If he's the last one standing on his team, he falls.

How to draft

  • 2 pts; only with Mark-able Hashira (Giyu, Gyomei, Sanemi, Muichiro, Mitsuri, Obanai).

Game plan

  • Round 1 or 2: Final Resolve, before they can kill him.
  • Without Mark-able Hashira, Words of Resolve heals your team 2 a round.

Win with it

  • Never attacks. Words of Resolve heals every ally 2. Final Resolve: he knocks himself out and every Hashira on the team with a Mark card awakens it (Giyu, Gyomei, Sanemi, Muichiro, Mitsuri, Obanai).
  • Corps Master: Hashira get +1 DEF. With two Mark-able Hashira on the team he is at his best.
  • Fire Final Resolve early, before they can kill him.

How to beat it

  • Kill him before his special: if an enemy knocks him out first, nobody awakens.
  • He has 18 HP and SPD 5: two hits in round 1 usually do it.

Synergies and matchups

Corps Master

Kagaya plus any Hashira on one team: every Hashira on that team gets +1 DEF.

Same team: Giyu Tomioka, Giyu Tomioka (Mark), Gyomei Himejima, Gyomei Himejima (Mark), Sanemi Shinazugawa, Sanemi Shinazugawa (Mark), Muichiro Tokito, Muichiro Tokito (Mark), Mitsuri Kanroji, Mitsuri Kanroji (Mark), Obanai Iguro, Obanai Iguro (Mark), Kyojuro Rengoku, Tengen Uzui, Tengen Uzui (Retired), Shinobu Kocho

Final Resolve

Kagaya's special knocks himself out and every Hashira on his team who has a Mark awakens it. If an enemy knocks him out first, nobody awakens.

Same team: Giyu Tomioka, Giyu Tomioka (Mark), Gyomei Himejima, Gyomei Himejima (Mark), Sanemi Shinazugawa, Sanemi Shinazugawa (Mark), Muichiro Tokito, Muichiro Tokito (Mark), Mitsuri Kanroji, Mitsuri Kanroji (Mark), Obanai Iguro, Obanai Iguro (Mark)

Unlock: win the Story battle “The Ubuyashiki Estate”.
