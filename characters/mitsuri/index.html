Recharges in 3 rounds.

“I won't let anyone else get hurt!”

How to draft

  • 5 pts; mostly a filler. Prefer her Mark version.

Game plan

  • Catlove Shower on wide teams; Attack single targets otherwise.

Win with it

  • Catlove Shower hits every enemy for PWR−3 and goes straight through Defend, so guarding doesn't help against it: best against many fragile fighters.
  • Weak at 20 pts: her Mark version is the better pick.

How to beat it

  • Her area damage is small per target: stack DEF and ignore her until the end.
