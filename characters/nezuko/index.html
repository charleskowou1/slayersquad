Passive

Conquers the sun: the first sunrise burns her, then the sun never hurts her again.

How to draft

  • 4 pts, but only worth it next to Tanjiro (Siblings).

Game plan

  • Exploding Blood on round 1 against wide teams; then Attack.
  • Keep her alive: she's the counter-hit half of Siblings.

Win with it

  • Exploding Blood hits every enemy for 5, ignoring DEF, and demons it hits don't regenerate that round: best against wide teams and regenerating demons.
  • With Tanjiro: Siblings, they counterattack when the other is hit.
  • Weak on her own: take her for the pair, not alone.

How to beat it

  • Low HP; she drops fast. Kill her before Tanjiro to stop the Siblings counter.
  • She is a demon, but the sun only burns her on the first morning (round 10): after that she has conquered it, and she regenerates again.

Synergies and matchups

Siblings

On one team: when either is hit, the other strikes back at the attacker for 3 damage.

Same team: Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Mark), Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King), Nezuko Kamado (Awakened)

Other versions
