Gyutaro

DemonUpper Moon 68 pts

“Whoever made my sister cry, I’ll take everything from them.”

Upper Moon Six, born in Yoshiwara’s gutters. He shares one rank, and one life, with his sister.

Role: Area poison, half of Two as One

Play Gyutaro free
40HP

Special: Flying Blood Sickles

Every enemy takes 4 and is poisoned 2/round for 2 rounds.

Recharges in 3 rounds.

“Take it all from them!”

Passive

Two as One: with Daki on his team he gets +2 PWR. If one of them falls while the other is still standing, they get back up at the end of the round with 30% health (once each). Knock both out in the same round to stop it.

How to draft

  • 8 pts; with Daki (14 for the pair).

Game plan

  • Blood Sickles on round 1 to poison everyone, then Attack the weakest.

Win with it

  • Flying Blood Sickles: 4 to every enemy and poison 2 a round for 2 rounds.
  • With Daki (Two as One) he gets +2 PWR and they each rise once. Akaza is a good partner too (two Upper Moons).

How to beat it

  • Same as Daki: finish both in one round. Tamayo and Awakened Nezuko cure the poison.

Synergies and matchups

Two as One

Gyutaro and Daki on one team: Gyutaro gets +2 PWR, and if one falls while the other still stands, they get back up at the end of the round with 30% (once each). Knock both out in the same round to stop it.

Same team: Daki

Destined foe

Facing Gyutaro or Daki, Tanjiro may awaken the Demon Slayer Mark when down to 30% (rare, once a match). Don't count on it.

Against: Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Mark), Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King), Daki

Antidote

Tamayo's heal also cures poison, so she can undo Shinobu's and Gyutaro's poison on her team.

Against: Tamayo, Shinobu Kocho

Unlock: win the Story battle “Two as One”.
