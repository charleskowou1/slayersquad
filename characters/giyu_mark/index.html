Giyu Tomioka (Mark)

Mark ✦Water Hashira10 pts

“Don’t ever give others the power to decide your fate!”

The Water Hashira who spared Nezuko and sent Tanjiro to Urokodaki.

Role: Team shield with Mark stats

Special: Dead Calm

“Water Breathing, Eleventh Form.”

Passive

Mark awakened: boosted stats and the special recharges 1 round faster.

How to draft

  • 10 pts; a top pick at 30 points, with Rengoku or Gyomei.

Game plan

  • Same as Giyu, but Dead Calm comes back a round sooner: you can cover two of their big rounds out of three.

Win with it

  • Same Dead Calm, recharges a round faster, with Mark stats. Among the best cards at 30 pts.
  • Pair him with Rengoku: one shields, the other hits hard.

How to beat it

  • Bait Dead Calm with a cheap special, then fire your real one the next round.
  • Gyutaro's poison and steady pressure beat him most often at 20 pts.

Synergies and matchups

Old Friends

Giyu and Sabito on one team: Giyu gets +2 PWR and +1 DEF, Sabito +1 SPD.

Same team: Giyu Tomioka, Sabito

Hashira

Two or more Hashira on one team: each Hashira gets +1 PWR.

Mentor

Same team: Sakonji Urokodaki, Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Mark), Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King), Giyu Tomioka, Sabito

Kagaya plus any Hashira on one team: every Hashira on that team gets +1 DEF.
