How to draft

  • 7 pts; pair with Giyu for protection.

Game plan

  • Flame Tiger first (PWR+4), Attack while it recharges.
  • Ninth Form (PWR+10, ignores DEF) on their biggest threat.

Win with it

  • Flame Tiger (PWR+4), then Ninth Form: Rengoku (PWR+10, ignores DEF) the next time: the second one is a finisher, save it for the big target.
  • Pair him with Giyu ✦: Dead Calm keeps him alive until Ninth Form is ready.

How to beat it

  • Track which special is next: his Ninth Form is the dangerous one. Defend or hide its likely target.
  • Hantengu and Doma beat him most often (multiple bodies, regeneration).

Synergies and matchups
