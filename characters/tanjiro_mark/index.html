Tanjiro with the Demon Slayer Mark. He learns the Transparent World, seeing inside his enemies’ bodies, then the Selfless State, where even Akaza can’t sense him.

Role: Burst, then untouchable support

Second special: Selfless State

Once per match. Moves first. Until Hinokami Kagura is ready again, Tanjiro can't be hit, stunned or moved by the biwa, except on a round he Attacks: striking reveals him for that round (his attacks ignore DEF). Once in the state he can Save a teammate: they take no damage that round. Then his Hinokami Kagura can't be blocked by anything.

Recharges in 2 rounds · moves early.

“No wasted motion. No anger. Just breathe.”

Passive

Mark awakened: boosted stats and the special recharges 1 round faster.

How to draft

  • 9 pts: strong at 30 points beside another Mark (+1 SPD).

Game plan

  • Fire Hinokami early to open the Selfless State.
  • In the state: Save the teammate about to fall; Attack only to finish someone (it reveals him).
  • When Hinokami is back, it can't be blocked: aim it at their best fighter.

Win with it

  • After his first Hinokami Kagura he gets the Selfless State once (after you've won the story battle Destructive Death): nothing can hit him until Hinokami is ready again, and the next Hinokami can't be blocked by anything.
  • In the state, use Save on a teammate who is about to fall, and only Attack when you need the damage (attacking reveals him).
  • A strong 30-pt card: a Mark, so with another Mark the team gets +1 SPD.

How to beat it

  • Hit him hard before his first Hinokami lands: the Selfless State only opens after it.
  • While he is in the state, ignore him and kill his teammates; he can only Save one of them per state.
  • Count the rounds: when his Hinokami comes back it can't be blocked, so Defend or hide your weakest fighter that round.

Synergies and matchups

Kamaboko Squad

Any two of Tanjiro, Zenitsu and Inosuke on one team: those two get +1 SPD. All three: the whole team gets +2 SPD.

Same team: Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King), Zenitsu Agatsuma, Inosuke Hashibira

Siblings

On one team: when either is hit, the other strikes back at the attacker for 3 damage.

Same team: Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King), Nezuko Kamado, Nezuko Kamado (Awakened)

Sun Lineage

Yoriichi and Tanjiro on one team: Tanjiro gets +2 PWR.

Same team: Yoriichi Tsugikuni, Yoriichi (Age 85), Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King)

Set Your Heart Ablaze

Rengoku and any Tanjiro on one team: Tanjiro gets +1 PWR and Rengoku +1 DEF.

Same team: Kyojuro Rengoku, Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King)

Mentor

Urokodaki plus Tanjiro, Giyu or Sabito on one team: his pupils get +1 DEF.

Same team: Sakonji Urokodaki, Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King), Giyu Tomioka, Giyu Tomioka (Mark), Sabito

Destined foe

Facing Gyutaro or Daki, Tanjiro may awaken the Demon Slayer Mark when down to 30% (rare, once a match). Don't count on it.

Against: Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King), Gyutaro, Daki
