Passive

Transparent World: strikes twice every Attack and never takes more than 18 damage in a round.

How to draft

  • 18 pts: take him whenever he's dealt. At 20 points he leaves 2 pts (Murata or Kagaya).

Game plan

  • Sun Breathing first (demons lose regeneration for good), then Attack twice a round.
  • He can't take more than 18 a round: let him soak, protect his partner.

Win with it

  • The strongest card in the game. Strikes twice every Attack and never takes more than 18 damage a round.
  • Sun Breathing hits every enemy for PWR, ignoring DEF, and demons it hits lose their regeneration for good.
  • Against him, Muzan loses 3 PWR and 2 SPD and Kokushibo loses 1 DEF. With Tanjiro: Tanjiro +2 PWR.

How to beat it

  • Honestly: ban him if bans are on, or take him when he's dealt.
  • Make him waste turns: Nakime's Castle Shift sends him away for a round, Yahaba's arrows make his hits miss, Rui and Gyokko stun him.
  • He takes at most 18 a round, so many small hits are wasted: kill his teammates first and outlast him.

Synergies and matchups

Sun Lineage

Yoriichi and Tanjiro on one team: Tanjiro gets +2 PWR.

Same team: Yoriichi (Age 85), Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Mark), Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King)

Muzan fears the Sun

Facing Yoriichi, Muzan gets −3 PWR and −2 SPD.

Against: Muzan Kibutsuji, Muzan Kibutsuji (Final Form), Yoriichi (Age 85)
