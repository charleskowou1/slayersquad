Passive

Still the Strongest: strikes twice every Attack and never takes more than 12 damage in a round.

How to draft

  • 17 pts: at 20 points add a 3-pt partner (Swamp Demon, Kyogai, Tamayo).

Game plan

  • The Last Strike (PWR+10, ignores DEF) on their giant; Attack twice otherwise.
  • He takes at most 12 a round, so he wins long fights.

Win with it

  • Strikes twice, takes at most 12 a round (even harder to wear down than prime). The Last Strike: PWR+10, ignores DEF.
  • At 20 pts he leaves 3 pts: Swamp Demon, Kyogai or Tamayo.

How to beat it

  • Lowest DEF of the giants (5): steady hits land, but only 12 a round.
  • Kaigaku did well against him in the sim: stuns and splash damage.
  • Kill his cheap partner, then stall.

Synergies and matchups

Sun Lineage

Yoriichi and Tanjiro on one team: Tanjiro gets +2 PWR.

Same team: Yoriichi Tsugikuni, Tanjiro Kamado, Tanjiro Kamado (Mark), Tanjiro Kamado (Demon King)

Muzan fears the Sun

Facing Yoriichi, Muzan gets −3 PWR and −2 SPD.

Against: Muzan Kibutsuji, Muzan Kibutsuji (Final Form), Yoriichi Tsugikuni
