How to draft

  • 16 pts; only with demon partners, and not when Tamayo, Shinobu or Mark Tanjiro are in the pool.

Game plan

  • Blood Whips on wide teams: he heals 2 per enemy hit.
  • Win before the sun.

Win with it

  • Blood Whips: every enemy PWR−7, ignoring DEF, and he heals 2 per enemy hit. With any other demon: +1 PWR to his demons (Muzan's Blood).
  • He's the weakest of the giants, especially at 30 pts: give him demon partners for Muzan's Blood and keep Tamayo and Shinobu off the enemy team.

How to beat it

  • Yoriichi, Old Yoriichi and Mark Tanjiro beat him almost every time.
  • Tamayo cuts his regeneration by 2; Shinobu's wisteria ruins him if he knocks her out.
  • He's 16 pts: kill his cheap partner and he's alone.

Synergies and matchups

Muzan fears the Sun

Facing Yoriichi, Muzan gets −3 PWR and −2 SPD.

Against: Muzan Kibutsuji (Final Form), Yoriichi Tsugikuni, Yoriichi (Age 85)

Tamayo's medicine

Facing Tamayo, Muzan regenerates 2 less HP a round.

Against: Tamayo, Muzan Kibutsuji (Final Form)

Other versions

One version of each character is dealt per draft.

  • Muzan Kibutsuji (Final Form) (18 pts)

Unlock: win the Story battle “The Sun Itself”.
