“Hold still. This will help.”

Passive

Anti-Muzan Medicine: an enemy Muzan's regeneration drops by 2.

How to draft

  • 3 pts: the best partner for a 17-pt giant at 20 points; with Yushiro otherwise.

Game plan

  • Heal your carry when it's around half; cure poison when it lands.
  • Against Muzan she also cuts his regeneration just by being there.

Win with it

  • Healing Blood Art: heal an ally (or herself) 10 and cure poison.
  • Against Muzan she cuts his regeneration by 2. With Yushiro: Yushiro +2 PWR.
  • The perfect 3-pt partner for a 17-pt giant (Old Yoriichi or Demon King Tanjiro).

How to beat it

  • She's the healer: kill her first, before she undoes your damage.
  • She's a demon: the sun burns her from round 10.

Synergies and matchups

Devoted

Tamayo and Yushiro on one team: Yushiro gets +2 PWR.

Same team: Yushiro

Tamayo's medicine

Facing Tamayo, Muzan regenerates 2 less HP a round.

Against: Muzan Kibutsuji, Muzan Kibutsuji (Final Form)

Antidote

Tamayo's heal also cures poison, so she can undo Shinobu's and Gyutaro's poison on her team.

Against: Shinobu Kocho, Gyutaro

Unlock: available from the start.
