About Slayer Squad

Slayer Squad is a free Demon Slayer fan game you play in your browser, on iPhone, Android or a computer, with nothing to download. Draft a squad of Demon Slayer Corps swordsmen, Hashira and demons within a points budget, then choose every fighter's move in secret each round: attack, defend or unleash a special like Hinokami Kagura, Dead Calm or Destructive Death.

  • Story mode: fight through the story from Final Selection to the final battle against Muzan, with cutscenes, Mark awakenings and star goals.
  • Online battles: play friends with a code or invite link, or find an opponent with Quick Match.
  • Co-op and Final Form: team up against the computer, or take on Final Form Muzan and the Upper Moons.
  • Same Phone and Quickplay: pass the phone to a friend, or battle the computer on Easy, Normal or Hard.
