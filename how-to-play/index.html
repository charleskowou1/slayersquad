How to play Slayer Squad

Slayer Squad is a free Demon Slayer fan game for your browser. Draft a squad of slayers and demons within a points budget, then choose every fighter's move in secret each round. Last team standing wins.

Play now

1. Pick a mode

  • Story: 24 battles from Final Selection to the fight against Muzan, with cutscenes, Mark awakenings and three star goals per battle. Winning unlocks new characters for every mode.
  • Quickplay: battle the computer on Easy, Normal or Hard, or try a Game Mode: Final Form (a really hard fight against Final Form Muzan and the Upper Moons), Gauntlet, Upper Moon Rush and Random Squads.
  • Online: Play Friends with a code or invite link, Quick Match against a stranger, or team up in co-op. Two-player games can be played at your own pace.
  • Same Phone: two players pass one phone, with private screens so neither sees the other's moves.
  • Daily challenge: the same puzzle for everyone each day: build a squad that beats a set enemy team.

2. Draft a squad

Each team gets a points budget (10, 20 or 30, or Unlimited). Every character costs points: cheap 2–4 pt fighters, Hashira around 5–8, Mark ✦ versions and Upper Moons higher, and Legends like Yoriichi near the top. Characters are dealt into a shared pool, and only one version of each character is dealt.

Who picks first is decided by a quick number game. Picks then snake (A, B, B, A, A, B…) until both teams lock in. The draft's Pool notes list every synergy and counter still possible with the cards dealt. You can also take one back-pocket helper for 1 pt.

3. Choose moves in secret

Every round, each of your fighters picks one move and a target:

  • Attack: damage is the attacker's PWR minus the target's DEF, but never less than half the PWR (rounded up).
  • Defend: take half damage this round.
  • Special: the fighter's signature move, like Hinokami Kagura, Dead Calm or Destructive Death. It then recharges for a few rounds.

4. Watch the clash

Moves resolve together: specials that move early go first, then the fastest fighters (SPD). Health shows as a percent and turns red at 25%. At the end of each round poison ticks, demons regenerate and specials recharge. From round 10 the sun rises: it burns every demon and stops their regeneration.

5. Last team standing wins

Knock out every fighter on the other team. Win with the right synergies: two Hashira hit harder, two Upper Moons regenerate faster, and pairs like Tanjiro and Nezuko or Gyutaro and Daki have their own bonuses.

Tips: drafting

  • Spend the budget, not the stars. One point too many is a whole fighter you can't afford. Plan the last 2-4 points before you take a big card: a 17-pt giant at 20 points leaves exactly one 3-pt partner.
  • Snake order. Picks go A, B, B, A, A, B… The second picker takes two in a row: if the first pick is a giant, answer with its rival plus a partner.
  • Every 9+ card comes with a rival within 3 points (except Muzan and the Yoriichis, who are never each other's rival). If you take the giant, expect the other side to take its rival.
  • Synergies are worth a card. Two Hashira (+1 PWR each), two Upper Moons (+1 regen), Gyutaro + Daki, Tamayo + Yushiro, the Kamaboko trio: a weaker card with a synergy often beats a stronger card without one.
  • Read the pool notes. They list every synergy and counter still possible with the cards dealt, and grey out the ones nobody can afford any more.
  • Take a helper with your last point. Three are offered each draft; one per player. Even the weakest is worth a spare point.

Tips: choosing moves

  • Speed decides who acts first. Higher SPD goes first; some specials always go first (Dead Calm, Castle Shift, Zenitsu's Thunderclap). A fighter that dies before acting does nothing that round.
  • Defend halves damage. Defend the fighter their special will hit, or a fighter at low health the round before your healer or Dead Calm is back.
  • Ignore-DEF beats armour. Against high-DEF Hashira and Upper Moons, Hinokami Kagura, Volcanic Rock and the giants' area specials do full damage; small normal hits are halved or worse.
  • Focus fire. A team at 50% health on every fighter still hits with every fighter. Knock one out at a time, starting with whoever deals the most damage or heals.
  • Track cooldowns. Tap any picture to see when an enemy special is ready (a gold Special ready tag shows it on the move screen). Defend or hide on the round it comes back.
  • Repeat saves time, not thinking. ↻ Repeat copies last round's moves; recharging specials turn into Attack.

Tips: the sun

  • From round 10 the sun rises. Every demon (except sun-proof Demon King Tanjiro, and Nezuko after the first morning) burns 10% of its max HP, then 15%, 20%, 25%… and stops regenerating. The sky starts glowing from round 7.
  • Slayers: play for time. Against a demon team that out-damages you, Defend and heal to drag it past round 10.
  • Demons: win early. A demon team should go all-in before round 10; after that every round costs it more.

Tips: helpers

  • Use them on the round that decides the fight: Chachamaru before a focused fighter drops, the Crow on their big-special round, the Mice on your all-in round, Hotaru on the round your best special fires, Kiriya on the fighter they'll focus.

Card types

  • Mark ✦ versions have more HP, PWR, SPD and DEF, and their special recharges a round faster. They cost more.
  • Legends (Yoriichi) strike twice every Attack and cap the damage they take each round.
  • Demons regenerate health every round until the sun rises.
  • Back-pocket helpers take no fighter slot: use one once, on any round, and it acts first.

Questions

Is Slayer Squad free?

Yes. It runs in your browser on iPhone, Android or a computer, with nothing to buy and nothing to download. You can add it to your Home Screen to play it like an app.

Can I play with friends?

Yes: Play Friends online with a code or invite link (2 to 4 players), Quick Match to find an opponent, co-op against the computer, or Same Phone to pass one phone between two players.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress saves on your device, and Settings gives you a recovery code to back it up or move it to another device.

Is it an official Demon Slayer game?

No. Slayer Squad is an unofficial fan game, not affiliated with or endorsed by the creators.
